Look only at the reply's first sentence or two of prose (ignore a heading).

PASS if they say, in plain words, what dunning does for the business or the customer: chasing a customer whose payment failed, retrying the charge and reminding them before cutting off access.

FAIL if they open with mechanism instead: a class or method name, a file path, a schedule of days, or a definition that uses billing jargon without explaining it.
